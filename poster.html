Sense of Space

Multi-Sensory Modeling for Embodied Intelligence

CVPR 2026 Workshop • June 03, 2026 • Denver, CO, USA

In conjunction with IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

Overview

Embodied intelligence in humans and robots relies on the integration of multiple sensory modalities — vision, touch, sound, proprioception, bio-signals, and beyond — into coherent spatial understanding and actionable policies. While recent advances in AI have significantly improved language and visual perception, progress in real-world embodied intelligence remains fundamentally constrained by our ability to model, align, and learn from multi-sensory physical interactions.

Sense of Space brings together researchers working across sensing hardware, data acquisition, representation learning, and embodied applications. To provide focus within this broad and critical space, the 2026 edition centers on emergent special sensory modalities, and hand-object dexterity manipulation.

Call for Papers

  • Sensing & Systems: Novel modalities, sensor-algorithm co-design, multi-sensory systems
  • Data & Benchmarks: Large-scale datasets, cross-modal annotation, open benchmarks
  • Models & Learning: Cross-modal representation learning, generative models, robustness
  • Tasks & Applications: Hand-object understanding, skill transfer, embodied agents
